ABOUT US

GC Handyman Solutions is a professional, fully insured handyman and property maintenance company proudly serving Port St. Joe, Carrabelle, and surrounding Florida Gulf Coast communities. We specialize in reliable, high-quality residential handyman services designed to help homeowners, vacation property owners, and local businesses maintain and improve their properties with confidence.

Our services include general handyman work, drywall repair, painting, trim carpentry, door and window installation, fixture replacement, deck and fence repairs, pressure washing, property cleanups, storm preparation, and ongoing property maintenance.

At GC Handyman Solutions, we believe in honest communication, dependable service, and quality workmanship. Whether you need a small repair, seasonal property maintenance, or larger home improvement projects, we are committed to delivering professional results with attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

We proudly serve Port St. Joe, Carrabelle, Apalachicola, Eastpoint, and surrounding Gulf Coast communities throughout Franklin, Gulf, and Wakulla Counties.
