Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Port St. Joe, Carrabelle, Apalachicola, Eastpoint, Crawfordville, Saint George Island, and surrounding Gulf Coast communities throughout Franklin, Gulf, and Wakulla Counties. Additional nearby communities may also be serviced, depending on project location.

Are you insured?

Yes. GC Handyman Solutions is fully insured for your confidence and peace of mind on every project.

What services do you offer?

We offer general handyman repairs and maintenance, interior repairs and improvements (drywall repair, painting, trim and finish carpentry, door and fixture installation), exterior services (painting, deck and fence repairs, pressure washing), storm preparation, and vacation and rental property maintenance. See our Services page for the full list.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Whether you need a small repair, seasonal maintenance, or a larger home improvement project, we’re happy to help. Minor home repairs, fixture replacement, and door adjustments are all part of what we do.

Can you look after my vacation or rental property while I’m away?

Yes. We help vacation homeowners, absentee owners, and rental property managers keep properties maintained, clean, and guest-ready throughout the year with property checks, seasonal maintenance, cleanup services, and minor repairs.

Can you help prepare my home for storm season?

Yes. Storm preparation is one of our core services for Gulf Coast homes and vacation properties. Contact us to talk through what your property needs before the season.

How do I get a quote?

Call us at 239-595-8376 or email gchsolutions1517@gmail.com with a few details about your project, and we’ll get back to you to discuss it and arrange a quote.

Do you work with businesses as well as homeowners?

Yes. We work with homeowners, vacation property owners, rental property managers, and local businesses to maintain and improve their properties.
